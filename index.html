About MidFlorida Card Expo

The story behind the region's premier card event

Our Story

The MidFlorida Card Expo was created to bring together collectors, families, and fans of all ages in a vibrant, community-focused environment. Located in the heart of Port St. Lucie, FL, at the MIDFLORIDA Event Center, our expo combines hobby excitement with classic Florida sunshine.

What started as a small gathering of card enthusiasts has grown into one of the region's premier card events, featuring over 230 vendor tables and attracting visitors from across the state.

What We Offer

  • 230+ Vendor Tables – A massive selection of trading cards, sports memorabilia, and collectibles
  • Kid Zones & Activities – Family-friendly entertainment for collectors of all ages
  • Buy, Sell & Trade – A marketplace for collectors to connect and do business
  • Community Atmosphere – A welcoming environment for beginners and serious hobbyists alike

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the MidFlorida Card Expo

What is the MidFlorida Card Expo?

The MidFlorida Card Expo is a community-driven event that brings together card collectors, gamers, vendors, and families for a weekend of trading, gaming, entertainment, and fun. It features sports cards, TCGs, live events, and interactive experiences for all ages.

When did the Expo start?

The MidFlorida Card Expo launched in February 2026 with the goal of building a strong local collecting community while supporting charitable causes.

Where is the event held?

The Expo is hosted in the MidFlorida area. Exact venue details are announced for each event, so be sure to check official pages for updates.

What can I expect at the event?

You'll find a wide variety of activities including:

  • Vendor tables with sports cards and collectibles
  • Buy, sell, and trade opportunities
  • Gaming zones and tournaments
  • Pokémon Trainer Hub and community trading
  • Kids Fun Zone with family-friendly activities
  • Live entertainment, food, and more

Is the event family-friendly?

Yes! The MidFlorida Card Expo is designed for all ages, with dedicated areas and activities for kids, teens, and adults.

Can I buy, sell, or trade cards?

Absolutely. The Expo encourages trading and collecting. You can bring your own cards to trade or purchase from vendors on-site.

Are there tournaments or competitions?

Yes, the event often features tournaments such as Smash Ultimate and Madden, with prizes and more details announced before each show.

Do I need tickets to attend?

Ticket information varies by event. Some activities may be free, while others may require registration. Check event details for specifics.

How can I become a vendor?

Vendor opportunities are available. You can apply or get more information through the official MidFlorida Card Expo contact channels or social media pages.

Does the Expo support the community?

Yes. A core mission of the MidFlorida Card Expo is to give back by supporting local charities and helping strengthen the community.

Will there be food and entertainment?

Yes! The Expo includes food vendors, live music, and entertainment to create a full event experience beyond just cards.

How can I stay updated?

Follow MidFlorida Card Expo on social media or check the official website for announcements, schedules, and upcoming events.
